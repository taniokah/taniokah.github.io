# Hiroki Tanioka, Ph.D.

Senior Lecturer  
Center for Administration of Information Technology, Tokushima University  
Design-Oriented AI Education and Research Center

---

## About

**Dr. Hiroki Tanioka** is a Senior Lecturer at **Tokushima University**, affiliated with the Information Center and the Design-Oriented AI Education and Research Center.

His work spans **data science, machine learning, natural language processing, information security, sports analytics, and AI education**, with a strong focus on **real-world and socially responsible AI deployment**.

Key interests include:

- Privacy-preserving AI for public and educational sectors  
- Large-scale analysis of sensitive real-world data  
- AI-driven learning support and human-centered systems
